Our Process

The prep is the job.

Anybody can roll paint on a wall. What separates a paint job that looks good for ten years from one that peels in two is everything that happens before the first coat.

"We don't just finish the job — we finish it right. That's the only way we know how to work."

  1. Walkthrough & Written Estimate

    We come out, look at every surface, talk colors and sheens, and hand you a clear written price. No vague ballparks, no pressure.

  2. Protect Your Home

    Furniture moved and covered, floors dropped, outlets and hardware removed, edges taped. Your house gets treated like ours.

  3. Prep Every Surface

    Clean, patch, sand, caulk, and spot-prime. Exterior gets washed and scraped. This is where most painters cut corners — we don't.

  4. Prime & Paint

    Hand-cut lines and full, even coats with quality paint matched to the surface. Two coats is our standard, not an upsell.

  5. Clean Up & Final Walkthrough

    Hardware back on, furniture back in place, everything cleaned up. We walk it with you, and anything that needs a touch-up gets handled on the spot.

Questions

Before you pick a color

Are estimates really free?

Yes. We'll come look at the project, answer your questions, and give you a written estimate. No cost and no obligation.

Do I need to move furniture or take things off the walls?

It helps if you clear out small breakables and wall decor, but we handle moving and covering furniture and protecting floors. We'll walk through what to expect during the estimate.

Can you help me choose colors?

Absolutely. We can bring swatches, talk through sheens for each room, and recommend sample spots on the wall so you can see the color in your own light before committing.

How long does a typical job take?

It depends on size and prep. A single room is often a day or two; a whole interior or a full exterior takes longer. Your estimate will include a realistic timeline.

When is the best time to paint an exterior?

Paint needs the right temperature and dry weather to cure properly, so in Missouri exteriors are usually scheduled spring through fall. We watch the forecast and won't paint in conditions that compromise the finish.

What if I'm not happy with something?

Tell us. We do a final walkthrough with you on every job, and anything that needs attention gets fixed before we call it done.
